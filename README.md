# The Doomed Ducked

This game is about a duck vampire who wields a cursed sword that drains his health whenever he uses it. In addition, his health continuously decreases as time passes, forcing the player to act strategically and manage their resources carefully. To recover health, the vampire must drink the blood of defeated enemies. Throughout the game, players can find areas where they can upgrade the sword, making it more efficient and allowing them to defeat stronger enemies. The game is designed around resource management and strategic decision-making under time pressure.

This game is part of [MiniJameGam58](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jame-gam-58)
